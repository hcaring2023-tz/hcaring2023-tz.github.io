About the Foundation

Operating out of Chekeni - Mwasonga in the Kigamboni municipality of Dar es Salaam, the Caring Hands Charitable Foundation (CHCF) blends classic secondary education with essential vocational survival skills to foster economic resilience and moral uprightness.

Practical Vocational Modules

Carpentry & Woodworking

Equipping students with modern furniture design, timber joined structural setups, and specialized workshop security protocols.

Tailoring & Sewing Arts

Instruction in industrial textile cutting, apparel layout creation, dressmaking, and commercial tailoring enterprise administration.

Window Manufacturing

Specialized physical skills covering modern framing construction, aluminum installation methods, glass fitting, and architectural designs.

Secondary Education Framework

Registered and fully aligned with the National Examinations Council of Tanzania (NECTA) curriculum framework for Form I to Form IV certification.

National & Spiritual Values

Core curriculum framework emphasizing ethics, civic pride, and foundational religious values.

  • Elimu ya Dini ya Kiislamu (Islamic Knowledge)
  • Historia na Maadili ya Tanzania (Tanzanian History & Ethics)

Core Sciences & Math

Building systematic critical problem solving paths and practical engineering foundations.

  • Mathematics
  • Physics
  • Chemistry
  • Biology

Humanities & Business

Developing languages fluency alongside standard financial bookkeeping competencies.

  • English & Kiswahili
  • History & Geography
  • Civics
  • Commerce & Bookkeeping
